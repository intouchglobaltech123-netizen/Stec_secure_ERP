## Medium

- **Reports revenue is wrong:**
  - Invoices converted from quotations are left out because `linkedJobId` is never set.
  - Unbilled jobs count as "billed".
  - A CLOSED job counts as paid. — `ReportsPage.tsx:99-110`
- **"Collected" is calculated three different ways:** the Dashboard sums `amountPaid`, Payments sums payments, and Reports is job-based.
- **Technician hours are invented** when logged hours are low (`completedJobs*3.5 + 2`). — `stecsecure-context.tsx:1723`
- **The AMC report uses a hard-coded date** (`'2026-10-30'`) and includes inactive AMCs. — `ReportsPage.tsx:144-165`
- **Dates are stored in UTC** (`toISOString().split('T')[0]`). Between 00:00 and 05:30 IST they land on the previous day, and on 1 April that means the previous financial year. This affects about 15 places.
- **Forms keep stale data:**
  - The quotation builder pre-fills a new quote with the last one's items, date and customer.
  - The job-create form keeps the last job, including its quotation link.
  - The hold reason carries over to the next hold.
  - The serial return form carries over to the next unit.
  - The invoice "Cancel Edit" keeps the changes.
- **Duplicate customers:**
  - Quick-add creates the customer before validation, so retrying creates duplicates.
  - There's no duplicate check on phone or GSTIN.
  - GSTIN format isn't validated.
- **The job-create draft effect reverts the form** after quick-adding a customer and creating a quotation. — `JobCreateModal.tsx:110-187`
- **The product-used modal can record the wrong product**: its filter gets out of sync with the select. Quantity isn't tied to serials or stock. — `AddProductUsedModal.tsx:107-124, 188-204`
- **Stock counts are unreliable:**
  - Products that aren't serial-tracked can't be stocked (there's no quantity field).
  - Negative stock is hidden.
  - The "In Stock" tile disagrees with the product cards.
  - Barcodes aren't checked for uniqueness.
- **Repairs workflow gaps:**
  - The bench technician can't see their own ticket.
  - A bench unit can't be written off.
  - A repaired unit can't go back to stock.
  - A ticket can't be reassigned.
  - Status changes have no transition rules.
- **An enquiry can be cancelled after it has already become a job.** — `EnquiriesPage.tsx:611`
- **WhatsApp links are wrong:**
  - `+91` gets doubled.
  - Landlines are accepted.
  - An empty number opens `wa.me/91`.
  - The quote is marked "SENT" anyway.
- **Printing is clipped** because there is no print CSS. PDF pages cut through table rows.
- **Round-off can be off by ₹1** (`Math.round` on an unrounded float). Negative quantity or rate is accepted.
- **"Overdue" ignores payment terms**: it uses a fixed 15 days, and aging starts at the invoice date.
- **Quotation-to-invoice grouping matches on item name or equal total**, so another quotation's invoice can hide the "Raise Inv" button.
- **The "Use Sample Field Photo" button satisfies the mandatory before/after photo.**
- **URL parameters bypass the UI checks:**
  - `?create=true` and `?productUsed=` skip them.
  - `?previewInvoice=` and `?detail=` with an unknown id open the first record.
- **After logout the next user lands on the previous user's page** (`currentSection` is never reset).
